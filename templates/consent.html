Online Exam: Monitoring Notice & Consent

Please read this before you begin. You must agree to continue.

What is monitored

  • Webcam (face analysis only): number of faces in frame, whether your face is absent, head direction and gaze direction.
  • Browser activity: switching tabs, losing window focus, leaving fullscreen, copy/paste attempts.

What is stored

  • Only event records: event type, timestamp, duration.
  • No video, images or audio leave your computer. Face analysis runs locally in your browser.

How it is used

  • Events are shown to a human invigilator for review. Nothing is penalised automatically.
  • Flags are indicators, not proof. You can contest any flag.
  • Data is kept for {{ retention_days }} days, then deleted.
